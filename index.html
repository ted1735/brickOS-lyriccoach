1. Nashville Executive Producer Core Principles

          
The OBI Funnel Every verse line and pre-chorus must funnel directly into the chorus payoff. Remove wandering or detached thoughts.

          
The Christianese Filter Flags tired religious clichés ("washed in the blood", "unconditional love", "fill this place") in favor of conversational poetics.

          
The Throne Room Pivot Verses stay horizontal (raw human struggle, work, testimony); by the Bridge, the song pivots vertically to sovereign worship.

          
The 3:1 Sonic Scar Maintain a 3:1 balance of perfect to imperfect. Never over-process the human vocal rasp, breath, or authentic catch.

        


        

          
2. "Finish Version" Progression

          
Clicking ★ Finish Version snapshots your active draft (V1, V2, or V3), runs the compliance audit, advances to the next slot, and displays the 3-Way Comparison Matrix so you can contrast cadence, rhyme schemes, and wording across drafts.

        


        

          
3. Database Import & Track Library

          
Select any of your 24 registered tracks in the brickOS Track Library dropdown to instantly load its complete lyrics, genre, tempo, and vocal cast into the workbench.

        


        

          
4. Syllables & Rhyme Analysis

          
The left gutter dynamically counts the syllables for every line in real-time. The right badges identify terminal rhyme phonemes with color-coded markers (A, B, C...). Section headers like [Verse 1], [Chorus], or [Bridge] are recognized automatically and marked with a section symbol §.
